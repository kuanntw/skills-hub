# Taiwan National Development Fund routing reference

Use this file as a routing aid, not as a substitute for current official rules.

## Verification rule

When the answer depends on eligibility, investment limits, co-investor requirements, review procedure, forms, deadlines, or the responsible service window, verify the latest official information from Taiwan National Development Fund sources at `https://www.df.gov.tw/` and the responsible ministry/agency for special programs.

Snapshot date: 2026-09-28.

## Main routes in the current public framework

### Direct investment / larger applications

Current public NDF materials indicate that applications of NTD 100 million or more can use direct investment. The general principles publicly listed include co-investment with a lead investor, investment in unlisted/unquoted companies, and generally investment in domestic enterprises unless specially approved.

General investment cases are analyzed, reviewed by the Investment Evaluation Review Committee, and then submitted to the Management Committee for approval.

Official pages to verify:

- https://www.df.gov.tw/News.aspx?n=2E562B3DF04F19E9&sms=520BEA5A96670349
- https://www.df.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=866D41CE85655B07&s=DA762B6EE9CF6B16&sms=E352DDFFE92743FD

### Project investment / applications below NTD 100 million

Current public NDF Q&A lists multiple sector-oriented project-investment channels, including SME, strategic services, strategic manufacturing, cultural/creative, AI startup, green growth/net zero, and smart robotics routes. These are generally structured as co-investment with private investors and have program-specific conditions.

Do not choose a route only by company industry label. Match the actual project, use of funds, applicant status, investment amount, stage, and responsible agency.

Official overview to verify:

- https://www.df.gov.tw/News_Content.aspx?n=866D41CE85655B07&s=DA762B6EE9CF6B16&sms=E352DDFFE92743FD

### Startup Angel Investment Program

Current public NDF materials describe the program as supporting higher-risk startups and state that financial return is not the sole consideration. The current public page indicates startups generally established within eight years and with paid-in capital or actual fundraising not exceeding NTD 100 million, subject to exceptions approved by the review committee.

The public Q&A currently lists an initial NDF investment ceiling of NTD 20 million, with total NDF participation potentially increasing through later rounds, and describes co-investment rules and a simplified small-investment path under specified conditions.

## Routing output

When reviewing a company, return:

1. Most plausible NDF route.
2. Why the company/project matches that route.
3. Investment amount compatibility.
4. Co-investor requirement or structure.
5. Applicant/enterprise eligibility concerns.
6. Documents or evidence still needed.
7. Current official source and verification date.

If two routes are plausible, compare them side by side instead of pretending one is certain.
